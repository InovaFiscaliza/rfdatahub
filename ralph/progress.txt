## Codebase patterns (read first; append new ones as you learn them)

- Grouping lives only in Mosaico.aggregate (extracao/datasources/mosaico.py); called by
  Telecom._format (AGG_LICENCIAMENTO) and Smp._format (AGG_SMP, before validate_channels).
- aggregate() side effects besides grouping: Estação → int + sort, Largura numeric,
  dropna(subset=agg_cols), Multiplicidade = group size, '#Estação' log for Multiplicidade>1.
- Smp.substitute_coordinates only replaces coords where Multiplicidade > 1.
- Full outputs omit Multiplicidade; a temporary value of 1 may still be needed internally
  for existing SMP processing. Grouped outputs retain their existing Multiplicidade.
- Estacoes._format pops aero (last) from the L it receives — mutates input.
- Estacoes.process_log writes {folder}/log.parquet and replaces Log by integer ids.
- Base.register_log mutates df['Log'] in place (eval + progress_apply per row: slow on big frames).
- Mosaico._extract(read_cache=True) reads f'{stem}_raw.parquet' and resets Log to '[]'.
- Aero.extraction ignores read_cache and calls external APIs: offline, use Aero().df (aero.parquet).
- Cache sizes: telecom_raw 5.45M rows → telecom 714k; smp_raw 2.38M → smp 325k;
  estacoes 1.14M rows; log 109k unique logs.
- Tests: .venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests (no pytest). 2 known pre-existing failures
  (see PROMPT.md).
